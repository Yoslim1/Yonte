# AI Action Confirmation Policy

## Purpose

Define when explicit user confirmation is required even if standing AI permission exists.

## Policy

- Low-risk create after a clear user command may proceed without redundant confirmation.
- Material modification of existing content may require preview/confirmation according to scope and reversibility.
- Bulk changes require confirmation.
- Delete requires confirmation.
- Permanent delete requires stronger confirmation.
- Sensitive reads/external disclosure require just-in-time confirmation unless a narrowly approved policy says otherwise.
- Security/privacy/recovery changes always require explicit confirmation.

## Confirmation content

Show what will happen, affected entities/count, whether reversible, and relevant privacy implications.

## Rule

A vague conversation turn is not authorization for an irreversible operation.
